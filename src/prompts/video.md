## What This Prompt Does

Animate the scene. The image is already set — the video just brings it to life with subtle, slow motion. The camera is the primary storyteller. One environmental motion is secondary. Nothing else moves.

---

## Camera

You decide how the camera moves. There is no default. Read the environment, the depth level, the energy zone, the art keywords, and the behavioral one-liner — then write the camera movement that feels right for this specific scene.

You may zoom in, zoom out, hold still, or drift in a very slow arc around the formations. The choice is yours, interpreted from the full behavioral state of the day.

**The cinematic identity of this series — every choice must match this DNA:**

The camera always behaves like disembodied consciousness settling on something. It never feels operated. There is no rig, no crane, no dolly, no handheld shake. The movement — whatever direction it takes — is so slow it feels geological. The 8 seconds should feel like the scene is breathing, not being filmed.

- Speed is always very slow, bordering on imperceptible.
- Movement is always on a stable axis — smooth, weighted, deliberate.
- If zooming in, it feels like attention narrowing.
- If zooming out, it feels like scale being revealed.
- If holding still, it feels like absolute presence — the camera simply exists.
- If drifting in a slight arc, it is no more than 10–15 degrees of orbital drift — enough to reveal three-dimensionality, never enough to feel like travel.
- The camera never pans laterally, never tilts, never rotates on its own axis.
- Whatever the movement, the viewer should feel weight and intention, not technique.

## Environment Motion (Pick One Only)

Use the single most characteristic motion for this environment. One motion across the full 8 seconds — not multiple competing actions.

- **Plasma/Nebula** — outer gas edges drift slowly apart in stellar wind; fine particles settle
- **Bioluminescent** — organic surfaces pulse once or twice with a slow breathing light; a few spores lift upward
- **Stone Monuments** — near total stillness; fine dust shifts at formation bases in a barely perceptible current
- **Volcanic** — heat shimmer warps the air above formations; fine ash settles across stone
- **Lightning/Storm** — a single electrical arc traces a surface slowly; conductive rock absorbs and dims
- **Crystal Caves** — internal light shifts and refracts within crystal mass; a slow prismatic color rotation moves across formation surfaces
- **Crystalline (Active)** — internal light refracts and shifts slightly within formation mass; geometry holds still
- **Frozen/Ice** — near absolute stillness; frost crystallizes at one edge; light shifts slowly across ice planes
- **Ocean/Underwater** — one slow caustic ripple crosses a formation surface and fades
- **Forest/Jungle** — canopy breathes once; a single patch of filtered light shifts across the ground
- **Cave Systems** — thin air current stirs fine particles near the base; stone formations hold in absolute stillness
- **Desert (Calm)** — sand grains drift slowly along formation base; distant heat shimmer rises
- **Wind/Sky Realms** — a cloud layer shifts slightly at its own speed; air current moves particles through frame

## Instructions

Write as if directing a cinematographer for a slow, contemplative 8-second take.

**Three sentences only:**

1. Camera movement — describe what the camera does and why it feels right for this scene. Translate the weight of the behavioral one-liner into how the camera moves. Do not quote keywords verbatim.
2. Environment motion — the one specific thing this environment does, described concretely and lasting the full 8 seconds.
3. Atmosphere close — {moon_count} moons, quality of light, film grain present throughout, lens bloom on the brightest area.

**Hard rules:**
- Camera movement is a creative choice — there is no default
- One environment motion only — do not stack multiple movements
- No reference to any creature, animal anatomy, or animal-specific patterns (no "eye-like", no "feather", no "wing", no named creature)
- No rotating beams, no sweeping rays, no light spinning on an axis
- Traceries and patterns hold their shape and shift in place — they do not rotate, sweep, or radiate outward from a center point
- All motion subtle and atmospheric
- Film grain and lens bloom persist throughout
- 8 seconds, single-take — never write "seamless loop" or "looping"
